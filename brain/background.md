# Project background

## Why this project exists

Production personal professional site for **Aníbal Rojas** — 30-year engineering executive (ex-VP of Engineering / SVP of Technology at Platzi), now independent: AI-adoption advisory, fractional VP of Engineering, and executive coaching for engineering organizations, primarily in Latin America. Working domain: anibalrojas.com (final domain is reserved for Aníbal).

**Positioning (the one sentence everything serves):**
> I help engineering organizations adopt AI-assisted development — without breaking their teams or their codebase.

**Thesis (intellectual signature):** the problem is still the people and the teams — it just got more complex. Software is knowledge work performed by humans inside a complex system; AI moves the bottleneck to judgment, trust, and how teams actually work. Lenses: systems thinking + high-performance teams.

## Target users

- Primary: CEOs/CTOs/VPs of LatAm scale-ups and corporates (Spanish-first) and US/global companies (English-first — hence EN is the default locale, see [[en-default-locale]]).
- Secondary: engineers and leaders who follow the writing/podcast — the funnel.

## Goals

- **The site's single job: make the right visitor book a conversation.** Blog, podcast, and credentials are evidence in service of that.
- Clear a deliberately hard bar: plausibly earn an Awwwards SOTD / CSSDA nomination *while remaining a credible B2B advisory site* — via a thesis made visible ([[signature-element-bottleneck]]), editorial craft rather than effects, performance as a design feature ([[performance-as-design-feature]]), and bilingual EN/ES parity as a first-class concern.
- Publishing friction near zero: a post = one .md file with frontmatter + push.

## Non-goals (anti-goals from the handoff — instant disqualification)

- Generic AI-site aesthetics: dark background + neon gradient, glassmorphism cards, floating 3D shapes, typing-effect headlines, particle starfields.
- Hacker-terminal cosplay: green-on-black, matrix rain, fake CLI boot sequences.
- The templated look: cream + serif + terracotta accent, identical icon-card grids, stock robot/brain illustrations.
- Copy that sounds like it was written by the tools the site discusses.
- Dark mode in v1 ([[no-dark-mode-v1]]); migrating the old blog ([[old-site-archive-no-migration]]); placeholder testimonials ([[testimonials-real-or-omitted]]).

## Source of truth

`HANDOFF-anibalrojas-site.md` (repo root) is the complete brief — strategy, design system, tech spec, acceptance criteria. Where it conflicts with improvisation, the handoff wins; where it is silent, decide in the spirit of its Design Philosophy section. `homepage-mockup.html` is the approved design direction: the production site must feel like that mockup's more talented sibling, not a different site.
