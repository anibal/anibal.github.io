**Decided:** Aníbal does not do consulting in the traditional sense — no analysis-for-the-shelf, no reports as product, no slides. He **facilitates the adoption of Generative AI**: facilitate / coach / mentor / train, at different levels with different intentions. Core vocabulary: **outputs** (what AI produces; the *Fundamental Principles* series is their discipline) vs **outcomes** (what changes in the business); the horizon is **AI at the core of the workforce**.

Two documents govern every string in `src/i18n/ui.ts`: `reference/positioning-canon.md` (the arbiter) and `reference/narrative-map.md` (per-route job cards). They **supersede** the old "verbatim from homepage-copy v2, do not rewrite" lock (reopened by Aníbal same day).

Blast radius of the 2026-08-21 rewrite:
- **Diagnostic reframed**: product is alignment built in working sessions, not a report presented in a readout (step 3 renamed Report→Align; deliverables reordered alignment → roadmap-as-record → evidence). Name "Diagnostic" kept — flagged [REVISIT] in the canon.
- **Fractional VP stays** (Aníbal: "facilitating change is at the core of how I incarnate this role") — copy now names facilitation-from-inside explicitly; Plan→Embed→Fade arc kept.
- **Coaching**: already aligned; only a verbs line added.
- **About**: fully rewritten (arc / four verbs / refusals / not-for); no sentence shared with the homepage.
- **Principles**: full EN siblings of credo/myths/side-effects; essays stay Spanish with `lang="es"`; PENDING sentence finished as the outputs≠outcomes bridge; closing bridge block links the series to the services.
- Hook hierarchy: hero flag = "without breaking teams or codebase" (unchanged); argument = bottleneck + outputs≠outcomes bridge (thesis); signature = "boring technology" (About/Principles, once per page).
- The strikethrough device `<s>consulting</s>` / `<s>consultoría</s>` renders on the homepage services intro and About refusals (diff semantics, amber strike).

Alternatives considered: widening ICP to knowledge work broadly (rejected for now — engineering orgs remain the wedge, workforce is the horizon); deleting the Diagnostic (rejected — reframed instead).
